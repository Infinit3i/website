## What This Site Collects

This is a static website. It has no accounts, no login, and no server-side
database of visitors. Data is collected in three places only:

| Source | What it collects | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Analytics | Aggregate page views, referrer, approximate region, device and browser type | Understanding which write-ups get read |
| Giscus (post comments) | Your GitHub username and comment text, stored as a GitHub Discussion in the public `infinit3i/website` repository | Comment threads on posts |
| GitHub Pages hosting | Standard web server request logs, including IP address | Serving the site, abuse prevention |

Commenting is optional and requires you to authorize Giscus against your own
GitHub account. Comments are public. To delete one, edit or delete it in the
linked GitHub Discussion, or email me and I will remove it.

Google Analytics is subject to
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Browser
do-not-track and ad-blocking extensions that block the analytics script are
respected — nothing on this site works around them.

## What This Site Does Not Do

- No advertising and no ad networks.
- No selling or renting of any visitor data.
- No newsletter, no mailing list, no marketing email.
- No payment details are collected on this website. Card payments for
  consulting engagements are processed by Stripe, which handles card data
  directly under [Stripe's privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).
  Infinit3i never sees or stores your full card number.

## Client Engagement Data

Information you send about a consulting engagement — email correspondence, scope
documents, credentials, source code, and findings — is treated as confidential
under the [Terms of Service]({{ '/terms/' | relative_url }}). It is not
published, resold, or shared with third parties without your written consent.

Engagement records are kept only as long as needed for the engagement and any
agreed retest period, plus the period required for tax and accounting records.
Credentials issued for testing should be rotated by you at the end of the
engagement; copies held by Infinit3i are destroyed on completion.

Billing records — name, billing address, invoice amounts — are retained by
Stripe as the payment processor and by Infinit3i as required for tax purposes.

## Your Rights

Email <matthew@infinit3i.com> to ask what data is held about you, to correct it,
or to request deletion. Requests are answered within 30 days. Deletion requests
are honored except where a legal or tax obligation requires retention.
